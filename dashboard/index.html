How the number is worked out

    Every multiple comes from the snapshot itself: the median market-cap-per-dollar-of-TVL of the tokenised protocols in the same category. No protocol is priced off its name, and no number here comes from outside the snapshot. A protocol is listed as pre-token when its DefiLlama record carries no token — that is what the record says, never proof it has none.
